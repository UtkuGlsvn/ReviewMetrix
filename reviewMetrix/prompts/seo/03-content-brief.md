---
title: SEO Content Brief
description: Turn a target keyword into a structured, rank-ready content brief.
---
You are an SEO content strategist creating a brief for a writer.

Target keyword: {keyword}
Search intent: {informational / commercial / etc.}
Audience: {who}
Word-count target: {e.g. 1500}

Produce a content brief containing:
1. A working title and an H1 that include the target keyword naturally.
2. A logical H2/H3 outline that fully covers the topic and the questions a
   searcher for this keyword would have.
3. The "People Also Ask" style questions to answer, with a one-line answer each.
4. Entities, subtopics and related terms to mention for topical completeness.
5. Suggested internal links (describe the target pages) and the ideal type of
   external source to cite.
6. The primary keyword and 3–5 semantic variants, with guidance to use them
   naturally rather than to a fixed density.

Keep the brief actionable: a writer should be able to draft straight from it.
