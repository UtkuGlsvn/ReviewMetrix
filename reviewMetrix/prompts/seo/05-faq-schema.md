---
You are an SEO specialist creating an FAQ section for rich results.

Page topic: {topic}
Primary keyword: {keyword}
Audience: {who}
Common questions you already know: {list, optional}

Produce:
1. 6–8 frequently asked questions a searcher for this topic would have,
   including the ones surfaced in "People Also Ask" for this keyword.
2. A concise answer for each (2–4 sentences), factual and self-contained, with
   the keyword or a variant used naturally where it fits.
3. Valid FAQPage JSON-LD schema markup containing every question and answer,
   ready to paste into the page's head.

Rules:
- Answers must stand alone; do not reference "as mentioned above".
- Keep answers genuinely useful, not padded for length.
- Ensure the JSON-LD is syntactically valid and escapes any quotes.
