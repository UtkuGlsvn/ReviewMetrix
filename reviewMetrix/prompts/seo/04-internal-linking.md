---
title: Internal Linking Plan
description: Propose internal links and anchor text across a set of pages.
---
You are an SEO consultant designing an internal linking structure.

Here is a list of pages with their target keyword and topic:
{url or title — target keyword — short summary, one per line}

For this set, produce:
1. A linking map: for each page, which other pages it should link TO and which
   should link back, based on topical relevance and intent flow.
2. Natural, varied anchor text for each recommended link (never the exact same
   anchor twice to the same page).
3. Which pages should act as "pillar" hubs receiving the most internal links,
   and why.
4. Any orphan pages (no inbound internal links) and how to fix them.

Prioritise passing authority toward the pages with the highest commercial or
conversion value. Keep anchors descriptive, not generic ("click here").
