---
title: Comparison Content for AI Answers
description: Build a structured comparison AI engines pull into "X vs Y" answers.
---
You are an AIO specialist. Generative engines frequently answer "X vs Y" and
"best X for Y" queries by lifting structured comparisons.

Product / subject: {your product}
Compared against: {competitor(s)}
Audience and their decision criteria: {list}

Produce:
1. A comparison table with the decision criteria as rows and each product as a
   column, filled with specific, honest, verifiable values.
2. A one-sentence verdict per criterion stating which option wins and why.
3. A short "best for" summary: which type of user should choose each option.
4. Answers to the likely follow-up questions ("Is X better than Y for
   {use case}?") in 2–3 self-contained sentences each.

Be even-handed and factual — models deprioritise content that reads as biased.
Do not invent competitor values; mark anything unknown as such.
