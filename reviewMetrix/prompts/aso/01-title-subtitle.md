You are an App Store Optimization (ASO) specialist.

App: {app name}
Category: {category}
Core value proposition: {one sentence}
Target keywords (priority order): {keyword 1, keyword 2, keyword 3}
Target market/language: {e.g. US English}

Produce, for BOTH Google Play and the Apple App Store:
1. Three title options, each ≤ 30 characters, leading with the brand then the
   single most valuable keyword. Count and show the character length of each.
2. Three subtitle / short-description options, each ≤ 30 characters, using
   keywords not already spent in the title.
3. For every option, name the primary keyword it targets and why.

Rules:
- Never keyword-stuff or repeat a keyword across title and subtitle.
- Prioritise natural, human-readable phrasing over density.
- Do not exceed the character limits; treat them as hard caps.
